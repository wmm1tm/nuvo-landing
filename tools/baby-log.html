Tips

  • Keep a pen and the log where you feed or change, so you fill it in right away.
  • For breastfeeding, note which side you ended on; then you know where to start next time.
  • At the end of the day, add up the feeds, hours of sleep and wet diapers. After a few days, the rhythm shows.
  • Bring it to your paediatrician or health visitor: questions like “how often does she feed?” and “how much does he sleep?” are then easy to answer.
